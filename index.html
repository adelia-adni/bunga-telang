Delphinidin 3,5-diglucoside

Primary Anthocyanin

The primary natural blue polyphenol compound in Clitoria ternatea petals. In neutral water ($pH \approx 7$), it forms a stable blue flavylium cation network. Adding acid ($H^+$) alters its conjugated double-bond system to reflect vibrant purple/pink light.

Formula: $C_{27}H_{31}O_{16}^+$

Molar Mass: 607.52 g/mol

Color Spectrum: 580–620 nm (Royal Blue)

In Conclusion

Bunga Telang is much more than a beautiful flower. It is a versatile plant with significant cultural, nutritional, medicinal, economic, and environmental value. Its natural blue colour, remarkable colour-changing ability, health-promoting compounds, and ecological benefits make it one of the most unique and useful plants found in tropical regions. As awareness of sustainable living continues to increase, Bunga Telang is likely to remain an important plant for future generations.
